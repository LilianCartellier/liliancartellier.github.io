As my first professionnal gameplay programming experience, I joined Eugen Systems on the final development months of Act of Aggression, a real-time strategy game set in the mordern era. On this project I was able to leverage my skills to understand and patch a large C++ codebase based on a custom engine, fixing or improving various parts of the game, including enemy AI behavior.
