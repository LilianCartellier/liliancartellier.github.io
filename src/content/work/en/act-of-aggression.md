As my first professional gameplay programming experience, I joined Eugen Systems during the final months of development on Act of Aggression, a real-time strategy game set in the modern era. On this project, I leveraged my skills to understand and patch a large C++ codebase built on a custom engine, fixing or improving various parts of the game, including enemy AI behavior.
