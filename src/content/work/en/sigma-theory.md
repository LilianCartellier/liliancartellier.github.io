In Sigma Theory, you lead your country's intelligence agency amid a new Cold War in the near future, where technological advancements are accelerating at an unprecedented pace. I implemented most of the features for this project including :
- A technology tree with varied short- and long-term effects
- A system of random narrative events driven by the player's choices
- A secret agent management system featuring agents with diverse characteristics and traits
