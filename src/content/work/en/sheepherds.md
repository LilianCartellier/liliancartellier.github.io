In Sheepherds!, you play as a sheepdog guiding sheep to a barn across 23 playable levels. It is designed for up to 4 players in local or online co-op.
It's the first game from Ultimo Disco, which I had the pleasure of cofounding with four partners, and we made the game in two years using Unreal Engine 5.
As part of this small team with one other programmer, I created or helped maintain many features, including:
- A flock management system using a boid algorithm
- A rewind system available in some levels allowing players to go back in time along with all other actors
- A customization system allowing players to choose a dog breed, select a fur color, and apply an outfit
- Player progress management including an optional challenge system and an advanced difficulty mode allowing players to replay levels with new challenges and new flocks
