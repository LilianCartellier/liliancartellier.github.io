In Sheepherds!, you play as a sheepdog guiding sheep to a barn in one of the 23 playable levels. It is mainly designed to be play by up to 4 players, in local or online co-op.
It's the first game from Ultimo Disco, which I had the pleasure of cofounding with 4 associates, and we made the game in two years using Unreal Engine 5.
As part of this small team with one other programmer, I created or helped maintain many features, including:
- A flock management system using a boid algorithm
- A rewind system available in some levels allowing to go back in time along with every other actors
- A customization system allowing players to choose which dog race they want to play and to pick an outfit
- Player progress management including an optionnal challenge system and an advanced difficulty mode allowing players to replay levels with new challenges and new flocks
