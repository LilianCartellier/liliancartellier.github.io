Developed in 48 hours during GMTK Jam 2023, with the theme "Roles Reversed", Pushy & Floaty is a puzzle game where you control two characters with different abilities at the same time, and often have to switch their positions to take advantage of their unique strengths. As the sole programmer on the project in a small team including two artists and a game designer, I implemented all the game mechanics and developed a tool enabling teammates to build levels without manual placement.
