Made during GTMK 2023 in 48 hours, with the theme "Roles Reversed", Pushy & Floaty is a puzzle game where you play two characters with different abilities at the same time, and often have to switch their positions to take advantage of their unique strengths. As sole programmer on the project in a small team including two artists and a game designer, I implemented all the game mechanics and made a tool allowing my teammates to quickly create levels without placing elements by hand.
