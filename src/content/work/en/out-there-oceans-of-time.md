Out There: Oceans of Time is a survival game where you have to carefully manage your resources and crew while traveling through the galaxy. As the main programmer on the project on Unity, I was involved in every step from first prototypes to post-launch updates. Features I worked on include :
- Procedural generation of a galaxy
- Implementation of branching random events with multiple choices
- Implementation of narrative branches with short-term and long-term consequences
- Procedural generation of expeditions on planets
